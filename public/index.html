Citizen Guide

Waste Segregation Awareness

Source segregation is the foundation of a clean city. Sort your household waste into the correct bins to enable circular recycling.

Green Bin

Wet Waste (Organic)

Biodegradable organic waste from kitchens and gardens. Decomposes naturally into organic compost and clean bio-methane gas.

Includes:
  • Vegetable & fruit peels
  • Cooked leftover food & tea leaves
  • Eggshells & coffee grounds
  • Garden trimmings & leaves
💡 Tip: Never line your wet waste bin with single-use plastic bags. Use newspaper or compostable liners.
Blue Bin

Dry Waste (Recyclable)

Inorganic materials that do not decay and can be processed, cleaned, and remanufactured at city material recovery facilities.

Includes:
  • Paper, cartons & magazines
  • Plastic containers & rinsed bottles
  • Glass bottles & jars
  • Aluminium cans & clean metal foils
💡 Tip: Always rinse milk pouches and food plastic containers before placing them in the dry bin.
Red / Yellow Bin

Hazardous Waste (Toxic)

Substances containing toxins, heavy metals, or infectious bio-matter that present severe dangers if dumped into regular landfills.

Includes:
  • Used batteries & mercury thermometers
  • Fluorescent tubes & CFL bulbs
  • Expired medicines & medical syringes
  • Chemical paints, solvents & insecticides
💡 Tip: Wrap sharp blades and needles securely in paper and hand them directly to municipal hazardous collectors.
